For parents & families

A great career doesn't have to mean big debt.

Start in high school

Dual Enrollment

Georgia high school students can take college courses — at TCSG colleges and USG institutions — and earn credit toward a certificate or degree before graduation, with tuition covered for eligible courses.

Technical college

HOPE Grant & HOPE Career Grant

The HOPE Grant helps pay tuition for TCSG certificates and diplomas. The HOPE Career Grant can cover the rest of tuition for programs in high-demand fields like welding, nursing, CDL, and IT.

University

HOPE & Zell Miller Scholarships

Students who meet GPA and course requirements can receive the HOPE Scholarship, and top students can earn the Zell Miller Scholarship, at Georgia public colleges and universities.

Apply with confidence

Georgia Match

Georgia Match proactively lets many seniors know they've been admitted to participating public colleges and universities. Check with your school counselor and GAfutures.org.

Earn while you learn

Apprenticeships

Many trades — electricians, plumbers, maintenance technicians, lineworkers — let students get paid while training on the job, often with a TCSG college partner.

Stack it up

Start short, go far

A CNA can become an LPN, then an RN, then a nurse practitioner. Credentials stack — students can start working sooner and keep climbing.
