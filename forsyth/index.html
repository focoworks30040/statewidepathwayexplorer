For parents & families

A great career doesn't have to mean big debt.

Start in high school

CTAE pathways

Every Forsyth County high school offers career, technical, and agricultural education (CTAE) pathways. Alliance Academy for Innovation focuses on them, with pathways like mechatronics, cybersecurity, EMS, and drones.

College credit early

Dual Enrollment

Juniors and seniors can take college courses at Lanier Technical College or the University of North Georgia and earn credit before graduation, with tuition covered for eligible courses.

Technical college

HOPE Grant & HOPE Career Grant

The HOPE Grant helps pay tuition for certificates and diplomas at Lanier Tech. The HOPE Career Grant can cover the rest of tuition for high-demand programs like welding, nursing, and IT.

University

HOPE & Zell Miller Scholarships

Students who meet GPA and course requirements can receive the HOPE Scholarship, and top students can earn the Zell Miller Scholarship, at Georgia public colleges like UNG.

Apply with confidence

Georgia Match

Georgia Match proactively lets many seniors know they've been admitted to participating public colleges and universities. Check with your school counselor and GAfutures.org.

Stack it up

Start short, go far

A high school pathway can lead to a Lanier Tech certificate, then a degree at UNG. Medical assistant can lead to nursing; IT support can lead to cybersecurity. Students can start working sooner and keep climbing.
