Dahlonega Plateau

Georgia's first wine region drawn entirely inside the state.

Recognized in 2018, this rolling upland in Lumpkin and White counties has cool nights and well-drained slopes. Cabernet Franc, Tannat and Petit Manseng do especially well. It also has the most tasting rooms in Georgia.

Upper Hiwassee Highlands

Higher. Cooler. Shared with North Carolina.

Recognized in 2014, these mountain valleys around Blairsville, Hiawassee and Young Harris suit hybrids and aromatic whites. Several tasting rooms overlook the lakes.

South Georgia

Where the native grape is at home.

Heat and humidity defeat European vines on the Coastal Plain, but they suit muscadine. Its bronze and black wines range from bone dry to lush and sweet.

Grapes to know.

Petit Manseng

White · Southwest France

Handles humidity well. Tropical fruit and bright acidity, made dry or off-dry.

Norton / Cynthiana

Red · Native American

Grown in the Southeast for generations. Dark berries, plum and firm acidity. It ages better than you'd expect.

Tannat

Red · Southwest France

Big tannin and deep color. Mountain vineyards in Rabun County give it a peppery edge.

Cabernet Franc

Red · Bordeaux

It ripens before Cabernet Sauvignon, so it is usually picked before the autumn rains. Red fruit, herbs and graphite.

Muscadine

Red & bronze · Native

Vitis rotundifolia, the native grape of the Southeast. Musky, floral and grapey. Scuppernong is its best-known bronze form.

A weekend in the hills.

Day 1 · Morning

Dahlonega

Start at Three Sisters for Cynthiana, then go to Wolf Mountain for sparkling wine and lunch. End at Frogtown with a red blend on the deck.

Day 1 · Evening

Cleveland & Helen

Take the cave tour at Yonah Mountain, then have dinner in Helen. Stop at Habersham, one of Georgia's oldest wineries, for muscadine.

Day 2

Tiger, Rabun County

Taste Petit Manseng at Tiger Mountain and Tannat at Stonewall Creek. Drive home past Tallulah Gorge.

Three tasting rooms a day keeps the drive easy.

Book a designated driver or a tour shuttle before you go.
